\subsection{A metadata-only composition holdout for ODAC25}
\label{sec:odac25}

We next apply the framework to a composition holdout in the atomistic ODAC25
release \citep{sriram2026odac25}. The aim is to separate compositions at the
original training and validation counts using only metadata. Reading names,
formulas and multiplicities from pinned ColabFit Parquet snapshots requires a
transfer of \ODACTransferMB{} MB of production payload. After restoring
multiplicities, the \ODACUniqueRows{} retained rows represent \ODACFrames{}
configurations. These totals exactly match the published filtered training and
validation counts. Small pilot and documentation requests are additional;
coordinates, forces and energy labels are not read. Conservative rules based on
family names, shared configuration aliases and identical compositions define
\ODACGroups{} indivisible components. A versioned weighted-factor adapter then
constructs a two-fold holdout at exactly \ODACTrain{} training and \ODACVal{}
validation configurations.

On the recorded elemental-fraction vectors, the count-normalized global overlap
falls from \ODACOriginalGlobal{} for the original split to \ODACLowGlobal{}
after low-rank optimization. Mean nearest-training similarity falls from
\ODACOriginalNN{} to \ODACLowNN{} (Table~\ref{tab:odac25}). The same-group
feasible initialization already reduces overlap, but further optimization adds
substantial separation. A dense-kernel control is competitive, so this small
representative problem does not establish a low-rank speed advantage. Thus, the
millions of configurations describe how many records receive an assignment;
optimization operates on \ODACGroups{} representatives.

The resulting boundary is a strong composition-shift test. Phosphorus occurs in
\ODACPhosphorusVal\% of validation configurations versus \ODACPhosphorusTrain\%
of training configurations for seed zero, and two components contribute
\ODACTopTwo\% of validation weight. Zero identical- composition overlap follows
from the imposed grouping contract. These results demonstrate that the
specified holdout can be constructed using a small metadata transfer. Its
relevance to prediction and deployment remains untested. The ODAC25 section of
the Supplementary Information gives the weighting, aggregation, source
versions, individual seeds and numerical checks.
