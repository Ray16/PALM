\subsection{A metadata-only composition holdout for ODAC25}
\label{sec:odac25}

The atomistic ODAC25 release provides a separate application of constrained
split design \citep{sriram2026odac25}. Reading only names, formulas and
multiplicities from pinned ColabFit Parquet snapshots transfers
\ODACTransferMB{} MB of production payload. The retained
\ODACUniqueRows{} rows represent \ODACFrames{} configurations after restoring
multiplicities, exactly reconciling the published filtered training and
validation counts. Small pilot and documentation requests are additional;
coordinates, forces and energy labels are not read. Conservative family-name
rules, shared configuration aliases and identical compositions define
\ODACGroups{} indivisible components. A versioned weighted-factor adapter then
constructs a two-fold holdout at exactly \ODACTrain{} training and
\ODACVal{} validation configurations. This case uses the frozen v0.1 factor
implementation and a separate exact-count group assignment, rather than the
primary v0.2.0.dev3 point-level solver.

On the recorded elemental-fraction vectors, the count-normalized global
overlap falls from \ODACOriginalGlobal{} for the original split to
\ODACLowGlobal{} after low-rank optimization. Mean nearest-training similarity
falls from \ODACOriginalNN{} to \ODACLowNN{} (Table~\ref{tab:odac25}).
The same-group feasible initialization already reduces overlap, but further
optimization adds substantial separation. A dense-kernel control is
competitive, so this small representative problem does not establish a
low-rank speed advantage. The millions of configurations describe assignment
coverage; the optimizer operates on \ODACGroups{} representatives.

The resulting boundary is a strong composition-shift test. Phosphorus occurs
in \ODACPhosphorusVal\% of validation configurations versus
\ODACPhosphorusTrain\% of training configurations for seed zero, and two
components contribute \ODACTopTwo\% of validation weight. Zero identical-
composition overlap follows from the imposed grouping contract. These results
demonstrate metadata-efficient construction of a specified holdout, while
leaving its predictive and deployment relevance open. The ODAC25 section of
the Supplementary Information gives the weighting, aggregation, source
versions, individual seeds and numerical checks.
