Controlled optimization revision — 4 November 2026
=================================================

The requested methodological controls are complete: 432 primary CPU cases,
36 nested ChEMBL dense/factor cases and 72 externally stopped equal-time cases.
The current package remains pinned to f7e0866. No new biological capabilities
or real-world deployment-performance claims were added.

The paper now uses the title:
Scalable Dataset Splitting through Constrained Low-Rank Similarity Optimization

Main findings and scope
----------------------
* Original-kernel quality is competitive with the same dense heuristic when
  initialization, capacities and search procedures match. Approximation error
  is reported separately from optimized partition quality and exact small-case
  optimizer gaps.
* Cost crosses over with dataset size. Small dense problems can be faster.
  At 32,000 ChEMBL fingerprints on two CPU cores, rank 256 averages 8.74 s
  versus 82.68 s for the direct dense arm, and 1.83 versus 12.04 GiB process RSS.
  Their original-kernel cut ratio averages 1.0004. These are implementation
  profiles, including construction and temporary allocations, not theoretical
  lower bounds for dense optimization.
* The earlier two-million-entity result retains its separate 32-core protocol,
  three seeds, 243.9 s median split-call time and 58.8 GiB peak RSS. Loading,
  warm-up and independent evaluation remain outside that clock.
* Matching DataSAIL groups removes the average global advantage seen with only
  count matching. Its coarsened objective still differs from the entity-level
  objective. CCPart controls retain native sizes and groups, with no silent
  sample removal or size repair. Native cost includes all threshold similarities.
* All 900 previous fidelity comparisons now include true-cut-relative errors.
  At rank 256, their maxima are 2.71% for ordinary similarity and 64.47% for
  cubed similarity. Accuracy of one kernel cannot establish accuracy of another.
* Assignment accounts for most of the measured global reduction. Extra restarts
  and refinement do not establish a consistent nearest-neighbour advantage.
  The global/local distinction is observed behavior, not an impossibility theorem.

Evidence and readability
------------------------
The main paper follows formulation, accuracy/optimization, scaling and matched
benchmarking. It defines the global and local measures separately, gives a
concrete CCPart example, and moves full grids and ablations to the supplement.
Per-case observations, assignments, input/source hashes, upstream CCPart source
and independent RDKit replay accompany the paper. The test suite passes 226
CPU-only tests. Technical completion is recorded in the new control revision's
verification/completion.json after all replay and reviewed-PDF checks pass.
