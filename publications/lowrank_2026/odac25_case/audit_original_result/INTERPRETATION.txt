ODAC25 result audit, 2026-11-03
==============================

The count-matched composition-separation result survives additional controls.
No new downloads or original-result edits were made. Saved script, input,
package-source and output checksums were verified in source_verification.json.

Actual recorded-formula evaluation (same frame counts for every arm):
  Original:            global relative overlap 0.969; nearest similarity 0.846
  Group-only start:    global relative overlap 0.825--0.873; NN 0.526--0.574
  Low-rank optimized:  global relative overlap 0.283; NN 0.194--0.196
  Dense-kernel control:global relative overlap 0.287; NN 0.154--0.164

All arms use the same source pool and composition representation. Group-only
starts retain the same immutable groups and exact configuration-count constraint,
and reproduce the saved optimizer initialization cuts. They use random linear
costs to obtain feasible assignments, not a uniform random feasible-partition
sampler. This ablation supports a contribution beyond grouping alone.

Independent float64 SciPy nearest-neighbour checks on 32 sampled validation
queries per arm agree with the Torch implementation within 7.2e-5 similarity.
The rank-256 proxy differs from the exact group-kernel cut by about 1.92% of
the returned low-rank cut. Count constraints remain exact and no group is broken.

Interpretation limits:
  * Zero repeated-composition overlap is enforced by grouping, not independently
    achieved by low rank. It must not be presented as isolated low-rank evidence.
  * Two of the three optimizer seeds produce identical saved assignments; the
    third changes nine group labels. These are not three distinct holdout cohorts.
  * The low-rank validation fold has 61--64 groups. Two groups account for 40.6%
    of its configuration weight, so millions of frames are not millions of
    independent evaluation units.
  * Chemical shift is substantial: phosphorus presence is 36.8% in validation
    versus 1.57% in training, and aluminum presence 27.9% versus 0.95% (seed 0).
    Atomic-fraction total variation between mean train/val compositions is 0.339,
    compared with 0.031 for the original split. This is a composition-OOD stress
    test, not evidence of improved deployment realism or target-error calibration.
  * At 2,050 group representatives, a dense-kernel heuristic is competitive. This
    application does not establish a low-rank speed advantage over dense methods
    or optimization of 37.7 million independent points. The dense control uses
    the same guarded heuristic and does not certify a global optimum.
  * Global scores and NN means use sampled configuration-weighted queries/pairs;
    their reported uncertainty is Monte Carlo error, not model/split uncertainty.
  * Family keys are conservatively inferred, and composition does not identify
    atomic geometry. Original composition overlap is not proof of identical
    structures across its folds. No energy/force prediction was evaluated.

Defensible current claim:
  A metadata-only, count-matched weighted low-rank pipeline constructs a strong
  composition holdout of the ODAC25 filtered release; optimization improves
  separation beyond the imposed grouping constraints. This is a useful
  application demonstration, with explicit chemical-shift and scaling limits.
