Publication readiness after ODAC25 integration
=============================================

Assessment
----------
The revised article is a credible computational-methods submission candidate.
The main contribution is compact dense-overlap optimization with explicit
capacity, fidelity and feasibility contracts, supported by current-code
comparators/scaling and a separately versioned ODAC25 composition application.
Technical checks and internal review support coauthor assessment. They do not
guarantee acceptance or substitute for author approval of the scientific claims.

The canonical starting manuscript is the October 26 v0.2.0.dev3 revision
(3f8f5a9), not the older October 22 manuscript in low_rank/paper/mlst_2026.
The primary method producer remains pinned to f7e0866. ODAC25 retains its
original v0.1 factor source and weighted adapter as an explicitly separate case.

Completed in this revision
--------------------------
1. Added ODAC25 to abstract, introduction, Results, Discussion and Methods,
   with a four-arm count-matched comparison in the main text.
2. Added supplementary source revisions, range-download accounting, original
   split reconciliation, identity rules, representation aggregation, weighted
   solver details, uncertainty units and per-seed observations.
3. Included grouping-before-optimization and dense-kernel controls. Zero formula
   overlap is attributed to the grouping contract; chemical shift and two large
   validation components are disclosed. Only two distinct low-rank assignments

   A relocated offline replay reproduces all reported formula metrics exactly.
5. Added generated-number/table validation to the article build, verified the
   ODAC25 journal citation against the publisher/Crossref, and checked PDF layout.

Remaining actions before submission
------------------------------------
A. Author input and approval (required; cannot be inferred)
   Supply author order, affiliations, corresponding author/contact, CRediT
   contributions, funding and competing-interest statements. Add ORCIDs where
   available. The present PDF deliberately retains explicit placeholders.
   Every named author should verify scientific claims, references and AI-assisted
   code/analysis/text. The disclosure identifies the assistance; it cannot
   establish that the authors have already completed that verification.

B. Choose the intended scientific claim and final venue (author decision)
   For a methods paper, retain the current formulation: representation-defined
   generalization boundaries, accuracy/capacity diagnostics, and measured
   quality--cost trade-offs. ODAC25 supplies a metadata-efficient composition-OOD
   application. The independent-entity scaling evidence comes from ChEMBL.
   MLST is a plausible fit; acceptance and novelty are editorial judgments.

C. Freeze the reviewed release and public evidence (required for reproducibility)
   Use the reviewed manuscript commit and stable release/supplement links for the
   package, per-seed results, exact assignments and source/data manifests. Verify
   the link a reviewer receives in a clean checkout. Expanded ODAC25 metadata
   may be regenerated with pinned column-range reads; the numerical result
   tables and formula/group maps are included for offline inspection.
   An archival DOI is strongly recommended for persistence and citation, but
   an independently minted DOI is not itself a universal submission blocker.
   The publisher permits supplementary-data sharing and accepts a DOI,
   pre-registered DOI, hyperlink or other identifier at submission.

D. Final human scientific read-through (required author responsibility)
   Check that novelty is distinguished from established Nyström/kernel clustering;
   approximate cut fidelity is distinguished from optimizer optimality; primary
   and archival source versions are distinct; original ODAC25 composition overlap
   is not described as identical-geometry leakage; and target/chemical shift is
   not used to infer prospective superiority. The new main table and SI contain
   the controls and limitations needed for that read-through.

Useful follow-up, conditional on expanding the claims
----------------------------------------------------
* To claim improved physical predictive evaluation, add a declared downstream
  adsorption-energy/force study on a bounded, identity-preserving subset, with
  the same model/tuning budget across original and new splits. Account for
  training-data exposure in pretrained models. Present target and chemical
  distributions alongside prediction scores. This is not required by the
  present metadata-only application claim.
* To claim ODAC25-specific low-rank speed superiority, benchmark a larger number
  of independent entities against matched dense/graph/group controls with common
  hardware, preprocessing and timing scope. Optimizing 2,050 representatives
  cannot establish scaling over 37.7 million independent points.
* Moving ODAC25 to the primary v0.2 implementation, broader representations or
  genuine chronological outcomes would extend the evidence. Their absence is
  disclosed rather than silently treated as completed validation.
