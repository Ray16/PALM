Low-rank improvement work, 3 November 2026
==========================================

Implemented: packages/lowrank is the canonical v0.2.0.dev4 development package.
The v0.2.0.dev3 manuscript producer remains immutable. Exact-count swap search
now batches candidate similarities, restricting evaluation to destination-fold
rows. Candidate coverage and integer count constraints are preserved; floating
point near ties may differ between backends. Sparse local-penalty and weighted
paths retain their previous solvers.

Validation: 237 tests pass, including 14 new dense-reference/block/tie tests.
The first unrestricted test attempt was interrupted after a native threading
backend created more threads than requested; the completed rerun pins two cores
and sets NumPy/OpenMP/MKL/Numba thread limits. The existing TBB-version warning
is retained in the test log. The development wheel builds successfully.

Paired timings (three seeds; two CPU threads; synthetic signed dense factors):
20-swap stage, n=2,000/rank64: median 0.1008 -> 0.0519 seconds (1.94x).
20-swap stage, n=10,000/rank256: median 0.5027 -> 0.2287 seconds (2.20x).
All six assignment hashes and final costs match.

Complete split calls (three seeds; exact80/10/10; rank256; four restarts;
two refined candidates; no leakage scoring; source/input records retained):
FreeSolv: median 0.2059 -> 0.1974 seconds (1.04x).
ESOL: median 0.4136 -> 0.2914 seconds (1.42x).
BACE: median 0.4319 -> 0.3842 seconds (1.12x).
All nine paired assignments match. These short timings are development evidence,
not stable cross-hardware speed guarantees or manuscript headline results.

Powered-kernel exploratory work:
A single sampled global-mean residual direction worsens cut fidelity on the
original optimized partitions in all nine tested cases. It is rejected as a
default. A three-fold-mean residual extension improves fidelity after
reoptimization in all nine dense-oracle cases, but FreeSolv's exact powered cut
worsens. No aggregate correction is shipped in the public package. The pilot
uses a dense oracle; scalable construction, held-out datasets, independent
accuracy audits, cost comparisons and optimizer-quality checks remain required.

Manuscript: the complete main article has received a prose edit for direct
scientific English, logical flow and precise interpretation. All equations,
citations, literal numerical values and result macros in the editorial pass
are preserved. No pilot results have been inserted into the paper.

Remaining broader improvement work:
- Develop and validate a scalable powered-kernel correction without worsening
  exact objective quality; retain independent returned-partition audits.
- Assess larger exact-count split cases and cost/quality trade-offs.
- Real dated/external evaluation is still needed for deployment-validity claims;
  the current manuscript makes no such claim.
- Author names, affiliations, funding/interests and scientific approval remain
  author responsibilities. The document is a review draft.
