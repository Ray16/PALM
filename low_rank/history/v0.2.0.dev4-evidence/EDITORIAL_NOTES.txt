Editorial revision, 3 November 2026
=================================

The revised main article includes the abstract, introduction, framework,
current-code results, ODAC25 results, discussion, methods and figure captions.
The supplementary article was rebuilt for consistency but was not rewritten.
Equations, citations, numerical values, result macros and the strength of the
scientific claims were preserved. A redundant standalone conclusion was merged
into the discussion during the preceding improvement pass. No new development
benchmark or exploratory powered-kernel result is presented as manuscript evidence.

Author attention:
1. The main text refers to a 1% capacity corridor without explicitly defining
   the denominator there. Confirm that the supplementary definition is sufficient
   or state the relative fold-target definition in the main framework section.
2. 'Family' denotes a known generator label in the synthetic experiment and a
   metadata-derived relation in ODAC25. These are separate operational definitions;
   readers should not infer that they identify equivalent physical relationships.
3. The study does not validate prospective performance on real dated outcomes.
   This evidence gap remains explicit and cannot be resolved through prose edits.
4. Author information, funding, competing interests and final scientific approval
   remain pending.
